# Filter by level or text

Click **E** and **W** to show only errors and warnings, or type in the filter box to keep only the lines that contain a text or match a regular expression. Lines keep their real line numbers, a stack trace stays with its error, and **Clear** returns you to the same place in the whole log.

A filter that finds too many lines (more than the limit in the settings) stops and says so; it never silently shows an incomplete list.
