# Search and jump

* **Ctrl+F** focuses the find box; **Enter** or **F3** goes to the next match, **Shift+Enter** or **Shift+F3** to the previous one. The count of matching lines is shown next to the box.
* **Aa** matches case, **W** matches whole words, **.\*** treats the text as a regular expression that cannot hang the editor.
* **Ctrl+G** focuses the go-to box: a line number (`1234`), a position (`50%`) or a byte offset (`@1048576`).
